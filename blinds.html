Overview


I built this project to explore how weather data could control my blinds. It checks the weather and time once an hour, then moves the blinds to one of three positions. I used OpenWeatherMap for the weather data and EEPROM to remember the previous position after waking from deep sleep.

The project builds on Kevin Darrah's code for his clear sky indicator.

Blind Positions


I use these OpenWeatherMap weather conditions.

The rules are simple:

  • Fully Open: Daytime with clear skies, few clouds, or scattered clouds.
  • Open Halfway: Daytime with any other weather.
  • Closed: At night.
