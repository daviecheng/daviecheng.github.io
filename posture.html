Overview


I built this wearable device to give a reminder when I slouch. It sounds a buzzer if slouching lasts at least seven seconds. I chose that delay so brief movements wouldn't trigger an alert.

I used the existing SEFR algorithm with the micromlgen library, following Eloquent Arduino's guide. I trained the classifier on 600 samples: 300 slouching and 300 non-slouching.

I lowered the Arduino Pro Mini's clock speed and removed unused LEDs to reduce power use. The battery lasts up to about 4.5 days under ideal conditions. A power switch and charging module make it easy to turn off and recharge.
